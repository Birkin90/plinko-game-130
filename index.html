How the PLINKO board works

The board is built like a triangle of staggered pegs. Each bounce sends the ball left or right, and that pattern repeats all the way down. The more rows you choose, the more times the ball has to make that split-second decision before it reaches a slot at the bottom.

Because each drop follows a chain of random bounces, results tend to gather around the middle slots, while the far edges land less often. That is why PLINKO can feel steady for a while and then suddenly produce a rare high payout. The shape of the board is what creates the rhythm, and that rhythm is part of what makes plinko gambling so easy to follow.
